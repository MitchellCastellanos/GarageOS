# Confidentiality incident response

## Intake
Any suspected unauthorized access, use, disclosure, loss or other breach of protection of personal information is escalated immediately to the Privacy Officer.

## Response
1. Contain the incident and preserve relevant evidence.
2. Identify systems, shops, people, data categories, time window and recipients involved.
3. Reduce the risk of harm and prevent recurrence.
4. Assess risk of serious injury/harm using applicable Quebec requirements.
5. If legally required, notify the Commission d'accès à l'information and affected persons promptly, and notify other persons/organizations that can reduce the risk where permitted.
6. Coordinate with affected shops when the incident involves data GarageOS processes for them.
7. Document remediation and closeout.

## Incident register
Maintain a register for every confidentiality incident, including incidents that do not ultimately require notification. Record discovery date, occurrence period if known, description, data involved, number/categories of people affected, risk assessment, containment/remediation, notifications, responsible person and closure date.

## Exercise
Run a tabletop incident exercise before launch and at least periodically thereafter.
