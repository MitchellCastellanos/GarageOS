# Privacy request procedure

Owner: Privacy Officer — mcastellanos@garage-os.ca

Use this procedure for access, correction, portability (where applicable), withdrawal/consent questions and privacy complaints.

1. Record the request date, requester, contact method, request type and affected shop/account.
2. Determine whether GarageOS holds the information for its own account or primarily on behalf of a shop. Coordinate with the shop when appropriate; do not disclose another tenant's information.
3. Verify identity proportionately before disclosing or changing personal information.
4. Preserve the original request and the evidence used to verify identity, while minimizing additional data collection.
5. Search the relevant systems/providers and document the result.
6. Apply any legal exception or retention requirement only after documenting the reason.
7. Respond within the applicable legal deadline and in the appropriate language/channel.
8. Record the response date, decision, data/actions provided and any follow-up.
9. Escalate complaints, suspected incidents or uncertain legal requests to the Privacy Officer.

For portability requests, provide information in the structured, commonly used technological format required by applicable Quebec law when the right applies and it is technically feasible. Do not promise portability for information created or inferred by GarageOS unless required.

Do not use ordinary support tickets as a reason to expose personal information to staff who do not need access.
