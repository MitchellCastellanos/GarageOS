## 3. Data residency — Neon Production in AWS us-east-2 (Ohio)

**Two questions, answered separately.**
1. **Is Ohio hosting prohibited? No — conditionally permitted.** CAI EFVP guide §7.1: personal information may be communicated outside Québec, or entrusted to an outside party, if the EFVP demonstrates the information would receive adequate protection, under a written agreement reflecting the assessment; if adequacy is not shown, the organization must refuse. The CAI's own illustrative project is cloud storage outside Québec. Nothing opened requires hosting in Québec or Canada. (Statute text unreachable — see §1.)
2. **Has GarageOS completed the assessment and safeguards for the Ohio transfer? Not yet.** The EFVP exists as a draft complete from evidence (`privacy-impact-assessment.md`, EFVP report v2) but is unsigned, and the Neon and Pusher agreements are unconfirmed. This is a **documentation/operator gate, not a hosting-location blocker**, and it applies **before the first non-operator personal information enters Production** (first real shop, or a Sales Demo prospect's contact details) — not at payment, not at the Stripe LIVE cutover, not at the operator-only dress rehearsal.

Other US flows (Vercel iad1, Supabase us-east-1, Pusher us2, Twilio US1, Resend US, Stripe US, GitHub runners) fall under the same s. 17 analysis, so the database is not the only transfer. Moving Neon to a Canadian region is an **option, not a requirement** (it would not remove the other US flows) and was **not** done. No CAI filing/approval is required.
