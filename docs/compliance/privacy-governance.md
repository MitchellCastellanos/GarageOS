# GarageOS privacy governance

Last reviewed: 2026-09-29

## Owner
Privacy Officer / Responsable de la protection des renseignements personnels: Mitchell Jeussef Castellanos Fuentes — mcastellanos@garage-os.ca.

## Scope
This policy covers personal information handled by GarageOS/GABAN Solutions in the SaaS platform, support, billing, security, imports, integrations and communications. Shops remain responsible for their own lawful collection and use of their customers' information; GarageOS handles shop-provided customer data to deliver the service.

## Principles
Collect only information reasonably necessary for defined purposes. Restrict access by role and tenant. Do not use shop customer data for unrelated purposes or sell it. Assess privacy implications before introducing a material new data collection, integration, tracking technology, automated-decision feature, or transfer outside Quebec. Maintain appropriate contractual, technical and organizational safeguards.

## Requests and complaints
Requests for access, correction, portability where applicable, and privacy complaints go to mcastellanos@garage-os.ca. Verify identity before disclosure or modification. Record the request, owner, dates, decision and response. When GarageOS holds information only on behalf of a shop, coordinate with or direct the requester to that shop as appropriate.

## Personnel
Access is limited to people who need it for their duties. Personnel with access to personal information must follow confidentiality, access-control, incident-response and secure-destruction requirements.

## Vendors
Before adding a provider that handles personal information, document its purpose, categories of information, processing locations, safeguards, deletion/return terms, incident obligations and subprocessors as applicable. Cross-border transfers require a privacy-impact assessment appropriate to the transfer before the information is communicated outside Quebec.

## Review
Review at least annually and after material product, provider or legal changes. The Privacy Officer approves material changes.
