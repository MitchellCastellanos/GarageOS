# CRM cleanup & repair fit — why irrelevant businesses reached the field map, and how it is fixed

Legend: **Verified production fact** (observed on production) · **Verified code** (read in the repository + tests) · **Estimate** · **Not verified** · **Owner action**.
**Production database access: none.** The Supabase connector available to engineering only exposes an unrelated auth/storage project (no CRM tables) and the Vercel Hobby plan keeps runtime logs for 1 hour. Every production COUNT is therefore **Not verified**; the cleanup preview below produces the real numbers.

## 1. What caused irrelevant businesses to enter the CRM (Verified code)
1. **Category trust without a sanity check.** Staging classifies by the Overture *taxonomy code*. A record labelled with a repair code (`automotive_repair`, `tire_dealer_and_repair`, `trailer_repair`…) became `PRIMARY`/AUTO-eligible no matter what its name said. Qualification V1 never looked at the name; V2 looked at it only for *generic* codes. Overture mislabels are exactly the examples reported: a charging station, a car wash, a bicycle shop, a tire manufacturer's head office, a recycler, a taxi-meter vendor listed under a repair category.
2. **Bulk human approval had no content guard.** `reviewCandidates(APPROVED)` only refused licence-blocked and `EXCLUDED`-tier rows (up to 200 per click). Anything else a person approved was imported.
3. **Import had no gate and the CRM kept no verdict.** `importApprovedCandidates` created prospects for every approved row; nothing recorded "is this a repair shop?" on the prospect.
4. **Assignment had no eligibility gate.** `buildAssignmentProposals` took every *unassigned, active, non-DNC prospect with a resolved territory* and distributed it. The preview was also **capped at 500 rows** (`ASSIGNMENT_BATCH_LIMIT`, `take: 500`, the returned cursor ignored by the action): "500 considered / 500 proposed / 0 not assigned" is exactly what a capped read of a larger, ungated pool looks like. **Yes: 500 is a batch limit, not the backlog.** The true backlog size is **Not verified** (no DB access); the new preview prints it.
5. **The Field Planner showed everything assigned to the seller.**
Which of the eight named examples entered through which pathway (legacy CSV, earlier V1 approval, current automation) cannot be established without production rows: **Not verified**. The cleanup preview lists them with names when asked (`Show business names`).

## 2. What was implemented (Verified code + tests: `repair-fit-domain`, `repair-fit-db`, updated V2/field tests)
* **One assessor** `domain/lead-radar/repair-fit.ts` → `VERIFIED_REPAIR | HUMAN_REVIEW | EXCLUDED` with reason codes, from taxonomy code, class reasons, CRM industry and name; **never from the name alone**:
  * *VERIFIED*: a known repair category (or generic automotive service **plus** repair wording; or CRM industry **plus** repair wording) and nothing contradicting it. Mixed services ("Garage Remorquage Roy") stay verified when the repair category and repair wording agree.
  * *EXCLUDED*: a known non-repair category, or a decisive non-repair signal (EV charging, car wash, detailing, towing, recycling/salvage, bicycle, parts retail, taxi equipment, manufacturer/corporate/distributor) with **no repair wording at all**.
  * *HUMAN_REVIEW*: category and name disagree; segments the commercial policy has not ruled on (motorcycle, trailer, dealer, marine/RV, tire retail, vehicle-brand names, possible dealer service); uncertain categories; not enough evidence. Collision/body, glass and diagnostics remain eligible per the existing V2 policy.
  * *UNCLASSIFIED* (service level, not stored): a hand-entered record with no staged candidate, no evidence and no contradiction is **not held** (keeps the manual CRM flow working).
* **Qualification** (all policies, V1 included): a decisive name signal blocks (`NOT_TARGET_BUSINESS`); category/name conflict or an unruled segment goes to human review (`CATEGORY_NAME_CONFLICT`, new, EN/FR). Policy options (`nonTargetBusiness`, etc.) still govern *category*-based decisions. A human rejection is never reopened. No policy is active in production, so nothing is re-evaluated until the owner activates one.
* **Review/import gate**: approving a candidate that is clearly not a repair shop is refused unless approved with `fitOverride` and a reason ≥ 10 chars; import rejects such candidates durably (`REJECTED`, note `FIT_EXCLUDED_AT_IMPORT`; a refresh cannot reopen it) and stamps every created/linked prospect with its fit (`AUTO`; an override becomes a `HUMAN` decision).
* **CRM**: migration `20261018090000_prospect_repair_fit` (additive: 2 enums, 7 columns on `CrmProspect`, 2 indexes, 1 CHECK; **no data change**). `repairFit` NULL = not evaluated.
* **Cleanup** (`/platform/sales/lead-radar/cleanup`): FIELD readiness funnel; **read-only preview** (dispositions, assigned/unassigned/FIELD-assigned split, protected records, by source, by qualification version, top reasons, optional business names); **bounded apply** (pages of 200, one transaction per page, resumable by cursor, idempotent, CAS so human decisions are never overwritten, audit per page + completion; needs an explicit confirmation); **review queue** (evidence, owner, stage, protected flag; confirm / exclude / reset with a required reason; reviewer + timestamp + reason stored and audited; durable across refreshes).
* **Protection**: a prospect with an opportunity beyond NEW, meaningful activity (note, call, meeting, email, demo, field visit) or an open task is never excluded automatically: it becomes `HUMAN_REVIEW` + `protected` with the original reasons kept, and ownership/pipeline are untouched. Nothing is deleted, archived, unassigned or reassigned; notes, activities, ownership history, DNC and suppression are untouched. Excluded prospects' pending route stops are closed as `UNAVAILABLE (FIT_EXCLUDED)`.
* **Assignment**: only `VERIFIED_REPAIR` (stored, or the live assessment when none is stored; UNCLASSIFIED passes) can be proposed or applied; `FIT_EXCLUDED` / `FIT_REVIEW` skips are reported; the apply step re-checks and its compare-and-set refuses a stale preview. The preview now **reads up to 5,000** unassigned rows (capacity accounted across the whole scan), keeps the first 500 assignments for the run, and shows the unassigned-pool breakdown (total / verified / under review / excluded / not evaluated), the number **deferred** beyond the 500-assignment batch, and whether more rows were left unread. Automation uses the same builder, so it is gated too.
* **Field Planner**: excluded businesses are not drawn, not counted as opportunities and cannot be route stops (`FIT_EXCLUDED`); review businesses stay visible (existing work is not hidden) with a "needs review" badge; the summary reports how many are hidden/under review. The 25-stop limit still applies only to route stops.

## 3. Owner procedure (nothing below has been run in production)
1. Merge/deploy is automatic; the migration is additive and applied by the Vercel Production build.
2. Lead Radar → **CRM cleanup** (Super Admin): *Load funnel* (where records are lost), then **Run preview** with *Show business names*. Read the proposed EXCLUDED / HUMAN_REVIEW counts, the protected count and the examples. Send the numbers back if you want a second opinion.
3. If you accept: tick the confirmation and **Apply (all pages)**. It can be stopped and resumed; repeating is harmless. Then re-run the preview (everything should read "already current").
4. Work the **Review queue** (assigned + protected first). Confirm or exclude with a reason.
5. Assign Leads → *Preview assignment* for the Greater Montréal territory: read the pool breakdown; confirm the batch (≤ 500 assignments per confirmation); repeat until the "deferred" line disappears. **The 500 proposals already previewed before this change must not be confirmed** (they were built without the gate; let that preview expire or Discard it).
6. Then the geolocation refresh (`owner-runbook-geolocation.md`).
