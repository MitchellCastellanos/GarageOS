## Scope and resulting flow (J–O)

Sales prepares and demonstrates the existing Shop, opens Convert to customer, confirms owner name/email, final Core/Pro/Complete and monthly/annual billing, and explicitly confirms retention of marked scenario records. The final plan defaults to the displayed demo plan. Sales sends/resends the localized platform activation email through the existing gated Resend client and platform email layout. Sales never supplies a password or card.

The intended owner activates a new verified OWNER account on that same Shop, or signs in as an existing OWNER already belonging to it and accepts activation without changing the password. Activation goes directly to `/admin/activation-payment`. Regular Dashboard/onboarding navigation also resumes this payment step while awaiting payment. The existing `PlanCheckout`, billing action, customer helper, Checkout helper, trial decision, confirmation and Stripe webhook/sync remain the commercial architecture. Server-selected terms override forged form values.

The unchanged contract is Core $199 CAD/month or $1,990/year, Pro $299 or $2,990, Complete $449 or $4,490; annual is ten months for twelve. Eligible owners see the normal 14-day trial, $0 today, actual future amount/date and applicable tax. Stripe captures the card; automatic tax, normal trial history and repeat-trial prevention remain in the shared path. A prior trial does not gain another trial through this feature.

**Exact conversion condition:** only shared Stripe sync may finalize. It must have accepted a mapped commercial plan, Stripe customer/subscription IDs and authoritative TRIALING or ACTIVE status, with existing customer/Shop binding checks satisfied. In the same transaction as subscription persistence, the bound SalesDemo must be AWAITING_PAYMENT and its activated account must still be the intended verified OWNER of that Shop. A conditional transition marks CONVERTED and finalizes onboarding on the original Shop. Sending/opening activation, creating Checkout, redirecting, incomplete/unpaid/PAST_DUE evidence and client UI cannot finalize conversion. Confirmation reports a recoverable pending error if commercial evidence does not finalize the demo.

CONVERTED is terminal for Sales actions and overrides. DB-revalidated impersonation loses the demo claim; the real owner has no Sales toolbar, and ordinary Subscription gates become the sole entitlement source, including normal PAST_DUE/RESTRICTED behavior.

## Additive schema and security

New migration: `20261001120000_sales_demo_conversion` (one additive migration, 50 total). SalesDemo gains nullable hash/expiry, intended owner identity, unique activated-owner identity, explicit scenario-retention flag, and unique Checkout attempt/session identities plus attempt timestamp. Existing rows remain intact. Unique nullable indexes reject collisions. A SQL check admits only 64-character lowercase SHA-256 hashes. Existing Shop/SalesDemo cascade direction is preserved: deleting the demo cannot delete the Shop. The owner identifier is deliberately not a cascading user relation; account deletion makes finalization fail closed.

StripeWebhookEvent gains nullable `completedAt`, defaulting existing markers to completed. New handlers explicitly create an unfinished marker. Completed duplicates are acknowledged; unfinished concurrent deliveries retry, and a five-minute stale lease permits recovery after a crashed worker. Application/finalization remains idempotent, so a retry after persistence cannot duplicate conversion.

Activation uses 32 cryptographically random bytes (256 bits), a unique SHA-256 database representation and expiry at the earlier of 24 hours (the existing verification TTL) or demo expiration. Token consumption and owner creation/linking are one transaction, with a conditional row-locking transition before account writes. Losing concurrent requests and unique-account conflicts roll back. Replay, wrong demo, wrong/expired/revoked token and expired demo fail closed. The token is bound to the stored demo/Shop/email, not a browser-supplied owner or Shop.

Tokens travel in the activation URL fragment and the intended owner's HTTPS action body. They do not enter GET URLs, access-log query strings, referrers, server-rendered HTML, persisted browser storage, marketing analytics, or database plaintext. Provider errors are not logged because they may contain the email URL. Only the hash persists. Local disposable fixture secrets are untracked and excluded from the PR.

Email collisions are checked case-insensitively: a new account is allowed; an existing OWNER in the same Shop requires that account's real login. Accounts in another Shop, SUPER_ADMIN, MECHANIC and VIEWER are never promoted or reassigned. Existing credentials are never reset. Existing unverified accounts retain the normal verification/login prerequisite. New activation itself verifies email and sends no redundant verification. SUPER_ADMIN and impersonated Sales sessions cannot set an owner's password. The platform's generic create-owner control cannot bypass demo activation.

Identical duplicate email requests reuse the pending result without sending twice. Explicit resend has a one-minute cooldown and replaces the hash, revoking the old link; changed details during cooldown receive an explicit error. A caught delivery failure revokes the unsent token and restores ACTIVE, allowing a fresh send. A process interrupted after persistence can be recovered by explicit resend; plaintext is never saved for retries.

Checkout claims serialize against the demo and persist one UUID. The shared Stripe helper receives a stable idempotency key, stable expiration and metadata identity; concurrent clicks/tabs reuse it. Open sessions resume, completed sessions return to confirmation, and only Stripe-proven expired sessions rotate. An old crash between provider creation and local persistence is recovered by enumerating customer sessions with matching metadata before rotating the attempt. Unresolved old attempts fail closed. Existing customer/subscription uniqueness and duplicate-subscription protection remain in place.

## Lifecycle, retention and analytics

PREPARING → ACTIVE is explicit Sales start. Sending activates ACTIVATION_SENT; authenticated owner acceptance consumes the link and moves to AWAITING_PAYMENT. Authoritative sync alone reaches CONVERTED. PREPARING/ACTIVE/ACTIVATION_SENT expiration records EXPIRED and revokes activation. All expired demos immediately lose Sales access/overrides, including already-open sessions. An already-activated AWAITING_PAYMENT owner can finish payment after demo expiry; expiration does not destroy an actively converting prospect. Sales workspace and authorized cron apply expiration. No destructive expired-lead cleanup is introduced.

Conversion only changes demo lifecycle/timestamps and Shop onboarding completion. Branding, logo/photos, shop/fiscal configuration, services/hours, Booking Page configuration and operational records remain. Native QA compares every persisted Shop field (except the expected onboarding timestamp) and retains an actual marked scenario, legitimate client and vehicle. Scenario retention is explicit; automatic deletion is deliberately avoided because live/financial relationships can make it unsafe. The existing guarded restart can remove a safely isolated marked batch before activation; it refuses unsafe links and never guesses synthetic data by content.

Existing platform overview, counts, subscriptions, MRR/churn/cancellations and commercial cohorts exclude every nonconverted SalesDemo, including expired demos and anomalous demo subscription rows. Converted Shops enter the same metrics once. Signup/cohort dating uses `convertedAt` for converted demos and `createdAt` for ordinary Shops. TRIALING remains excluded from MRR under the existing policy. No parallel analytics system was added.

Historical SMS keeps Wave 2's immutable `salesDemoOriginId`, independent of the SalesDemo's continued existence. DB trigger, allowance calculation, callback/reconciliation, fallback/retry/settlement and the lowest Stripe meter boundary retain the exclusion. Native QA attempts historical metering after both conversion and demo deletion: zero mocked meter calls. A new paid SMS on the same Shop reaches the normal mocked meter exactly once. No Wave 2 provider path was replaced.
